# Ball Party

Sports knowledge party game. Three modes:

1. **Individual** - play solo games
2. **Arcade** — play mini games with friends
3. **Party** — Mario Party style; join a lobby with friends, play minigames, earn
   points, first to a target score wins the whole game

Games planned:

- Auction team builder (individual)
- Cards Against Humanity (individual, first to 3)
- Higher or lower stats (individual, last man standing)
- 2v2 Wavelength
