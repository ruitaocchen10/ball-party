# Ball Party

Ball Party is a sports knowledge party game. It's a platform where people can come and play ball knowledge games on their own or with their friends. We have two ways to play:

1. **Individual** - play solo games
2. **Group** — play mini games with friends. We have 2 ways to do this:

- **Party Mode** — Mario Party style; join a lobby with friends, play minigames, earn
  points, first to a target score wins the whole game
- **Arcade Mode** — Play individual games on their own
