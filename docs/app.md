# Ball Party

Ball Party is a sports (NFL) knowledge party game and community. People test their ball knowledge solo or with friends, and argue about it in a weekly prompt feed. It ships as a Next.js web app (`webapp/`), a native iOS app (`ios/`), and a Supabase backend (`supabase/`). Technical decisions live in [architecture](./architecture/architecture.md).

## Surfaces

**The Huddle (home, `/`).** One feed with All, Prompts and Friends pills (`?filter=prompts|friends`). All interleaves prompt cards with friends' game moments by time; Friends is the prompts a friend answered plus those game moments (`friend_activity`, migration 0209: the last 30 days of multiplayer wins and new solo high scores (Higher or Lower streak, QB Season Builder by mode)). A prompt card is art across the top (a short banner, the same height for every kind, so a phone fits two cards at once) with a chip in its top-right corner ("Closes in 2d", then Completed, Locked or Resolved), then the title, subtitle, who answered and the button. A prompt card says which friends answered it ("Sam & Jo answered", `friends_who_answered`), never what they picked. Guests and signed-out visitors see only the prompts. The Huddle is a public feed of weekly prompts people answer and comment on: game picks, weekly picks, weekly awards, bold predictions, power rankings. Readable signed out; answering needs an account. Admins author and resolve prompts under `/admin`, which opens on a weekly calendar of prompt slots (see Admin calendar below). The prompt kinds are `TemplateKind` in `webapp/lib/foundation/content/prompts/types.ts`.

**Balls (BPP-39).** Every take, including a picks-only answer and a friend's list update card, has a thumbs up, a ball count and a thumbs down in its action row (the thumb turns into a football once you like it). A like gives the author a ball and a dislike takes one away; anyone with an account can like, only a friend of the author can dislike, and nobody can react to their own take. The net is added into the author's Ball Knower points, so there is one ball knowledge score across the leaderboard and profile. Rules in SQL (`react_to_take`, `take_reaction_summary`, migration 0216).

**Games.** What each game is, who can play it, and whether it is live is the catalog in `webapp/lib/platform/orchestration/catalog.ts` (`status`, `solo`, `group`). Rules live in one doc per game:

| Game | Plays as | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Higher or Lower | solo or room, 2–4 | [higher_or_lower](./games/higher_or_lower/rules.md) |
| QB Season Builder | solo only | [qb_season_builder](./games/qb_season_builder/rules.md) |
| Auction Team Builder | room, 2–4 | [auction_team_builder](./games/auction_team_builder/rules.md) |
| 2v2 Wavelength | room, 2 (co-op) or 4 (versus) | [wavelength](./games/wavelength/rules.md) |

Cards Against Humanity is not built: it has no catalog entry and no doc.

**Rooms (`/party`).** A host creates a room and friends join from an invite or a room link (`/lobby/<token>`; there is no code to type, and the token is long and random), and the host picks a game from the live room-capable games. Any member can invite friends from the lobby; the invitee sees pending invites at the top of `/party` and joins or declines (invites last 24 hours, in-app only, no push notification; they appear live while the page or Party tab is open). Each pick starts an **arcade** run: one match of one game. See [lobbies](./architecture/lobbies/lobbies.md) for lobby → run → match.

**Solo (`/solo`, and the sidebar's solo section).** Starts a solo run of any game with `solo: true`. Signed-out visitors get a guest session on web. Each solo game with an all-time best also has a leaderboard at `/solo/<game>/leaderboard` (see Leaderboard), linked from the "Best scores" rail with the viewer's rank. On iOS each solo game card on the Games tab has a Leaderboard button.

**Lists (web, `/lists`).** A sidebar item after Home. Each player keeps one living list per kind: Power Rankings (top 5, 10 or 32) and top 10s for QB, RB and WR. `/lists` is where you build them, `/lists/<kind>` is the editor (pick a row up, tap where it goes, save with an optional note and a switch to share the update with friends, on by default) with the list's version history under it, and `/u/<username>/lists/<kind>` is the same history for another player. A shared update is a friends-only card on the home **Lists** pill (also in All and Friends), with replies at `/lists/updates/<id>`. The server decides what can be saved and who can see it; design in [`docs/architecture/ranking-lists.md`](architecture/ranking-lists.md).

**Profile (`/profile`).** The header is a large avatar, the name and the username, with **Edit profile** and **Friends** buttons under it, then three tabs (`?tab=`): **Stats** (match history, knower highlights and rankings), **Takes** (the player's prompt takes) and **Lists** (the player's ranking lists on web, each at the size they chose; a placeholder on iOS). Another player's profile has the same three tabs. The Friends button shows the friend count and a badge for pending requests. Edit profile also sets the player's unique **username**.

**Friends (`/friends`, opened from the Friends button on Profile).** Accounts only; guests are asked to sign up. Friends list, incoming and outgoing requests, blocked players, username search-as-you-type (prefix match from 3 characters, at most 8 suggestions) and a shareable invite link (`/friends/invite/<code>`, which stays its own page and opens in the iOS app via a Universal Link). `/profile?tab=friends` redirects here. Rules live in SQL (migrations 0183 to 0191).

**Other players' profiles (`/u/<username>`).** Another player's highlights and match history. A private profile shows only its header to non-friends; unknown, guest and blocking users all look like not found (`get_profile`, migration 0186). Leaderboard rows link here.

**Leaderboard (`/leaderboard`).** Two boards, picked with underlined tabs under the page title (`?board=`, pinned under the navigation bar on iOS); a Friends / Everyone pill toggle, beside the window dropdown, scopes the board to Everyone or Friends (the friends board ranks you against your accepted friends), and a dropdown picks the time window. Accounts only, not guests or signed-out visitors, and a private profile still ranks. Ranking and ties are in SQL.
- **Ball Knower** (default): resolved prompt points plus net balls from other players (see Balls below) over the week, month, year or all time, with the viewer's own row and a 3-scored-pick floor (`ball_knower_leaderboard`, `my_ball_knower_standing`, migrations 0181 and 0182).
- **Gamer** (`board=gamer`): multiplayer wins over the same windows. A win is first place in a completed non-solo match with at least two players; a tie for first counts for everyone tied. Shows games played beside wins (`gamer_leaderboard`, `my_gamer_standing`, migration 0192).
- **Solo games' boards** live with the games, not under `/leaderboard`: `/solo/higher-or-lower/leaderboard` and `/solo/qb-season-builder/leaderboard` (an old `/leaderboard?board=<game>` link redirects). Each ranks every player's best solo run, all time, with no windows, and takes the same Everyone / Friends scope. Higher or Lower ranks the longest solo streak. QB Season Builder ranks the best season score, with a Visible / Hidden toggle (`mode=`) because the two modes are scored separately (`solo_leaderboard`, `my_solo_standing`, migration 0192).

## Admin calendar (web only, `/admin`)

Admin tooling, behind the same admin check as the rest of `/admin` (`prompts.is_admin()`); iOS has no equivalent. `/admin` is a Monday-to-Sunday calendar with a column for each day (US Eastern), drawn with FullCalendar: arrows move by week or month, Today jumps back, and a Week / Month switch changes the view. It shows four kinds of thing, each drawn differently, with a **Key** above the grid explaining them: **NFL games** (plain text lines with kickoff time and matchup, folded into "N games" in month view, with an NFL games switch to hide them); **planned prompts** on the day they are due to be posted (dashed for empty and draft, solid with a light edge for ready); and **published prompts** (open, locked, resolved, void, read straight from `prompt_posts`, so ones composed without a slot appear too) on the day they lock, plus a slim reminder on the day they opened. Empty slots are created ahead of time, never duplicated, each with its post time already set from a weekly cadence (US Eastern): game picks for primetime games only, noon on game day, locking at kickoff; weekly picks Wednesday noon, locking at the week's first kickoff; weekly awards Tuesday noon after the week's last game, locking Wednesday noon; power rankings Tuesday noon before the week, locking Wednesday noon; bold predictions Saturday noon, locking at the first Sunday kickoff (matchup tags then only offer games that haven't kicked off). Clicking a planned prompt opens Compose filled in for it; **Save draft** keeps it private, **Save and mark ready** queues it to open by itself at its open time, and the card moves to that day. A slot that is due within two days but still empty or a draft is flagged with the step it is missing, and `/admin/queue` lists every draft and ready slot. Publishing from Compose still goes live at once and marks its slot covered. A daily email lists the slots that need attention and is not sent when none do. Players never see a draft: the plan lives in its own table, and a prompt only enters the feed when it opens. Design, state machine and email setup in [`docs/architecture/prompt-schedule.md`](architecture/prompt-schedule.md).

## Safety and moderation

Every take and reply has a Report flag (not your own), and another player's profile has a Report button; a reported take is hidden from the reporter at once. Blocking a player hides their takes from you and yours from them. A short banned-terms list refuses obvious abuse when a take is written. The admin works the queue at `/admin/reports`: dismiss, remove the take, or ban the player. `/terms` holds the rules and sign-in links to it. Rules and tables are migration 0220.

## Accounts

Multiplayer needs a real account; guests are refused at `join_lobby` and `start_run`. Solo play works as a guest on web. iOS never carries a guest session. Guests cannot have friends or a username.

Every non-guest account has a unique username (3-20 letters, digits or underscores, case-insensitive): generated from the display name at signup, optionally chosen there, and confirmed once by existing users in a prompt. Settings has a **Private profile** switch (`set_profile_private`): only friends can open a private profile, but its name, username and photo stay searchable and it still ranks.

## Not built yet

- **Party mode** (Mario Party style: a run of several minigames with points and a target score). The data model reserves a `party` run mode, but `start_run` raises `mode_not_supported` for anything except `arcade`. Orchestration of the party loop is unwritten (`orchestration.md` is a todo in architecture).

## iOS

Four tabs: Home (one feed, with All | Prompts | Friends pills), Games, Party (rooms), Profile. The leaderboard is reached from Profile → Your stats → Ball knower → View leaderboard, and a picker at the top switches between Ball Knower and Gamer. Friends is a button on Profile, under the header, and other players' profiles open from friends and the leaderboard; the Profile menu keeps privacy, notifications, support and sign out. In a room, header tabs switch between Room (the invite and share-link buttons and the four seats), Game (a row of game cards: the host swipes to pick, edits the game's settings in its card and starts it; everyone else can browse the cards and read the host's saved settings, and the Room tab shows the game being played) and Scoreboard (wins since the room opened); the leave icon on the left of the header leaves the room, or closes it for the host. Room invites work on iOS too: invite friends from the lobby, and the Party tab lists pending invites. iOS also opens friend invite links and room links as Universal Links. Games on iOS are Higher or Lower (solo and live match), Auction Team Builder (live match), 2v2 Wavelength (live match) and QB Season Builder. Layout in [`ios/BallParty/Games/README.md`](../ios/BallParty/Games/README.md).
