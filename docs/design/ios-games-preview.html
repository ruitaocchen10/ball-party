Ball Party on iOS

A visual preview of the multiplayer games on iOS. Auction Team Builder is drawn from the shipped SwiftUI (AuctionMatchView.swift) using the real tokens from Theme.swift. Higher or Lower is also live on iOS. Wavelength is also live on iOS; its screens here are concepts built from the same UI kit and each game's rules doc.

From code mirrors what the app renders, laid out from the view tree. Concept is a proposal, not shipped. These are HTML approximations: spacing and fonts are close, not pixel-exact against a simulator.

From codeAuction Team Builder: today

Live room match, 4 players, Standard preset ($25 purse, 5 slots, tokens on). Scroll inside each phone: the bid bar sits below the fold on the first screen, and the clock and tokens are further down.

ConceptAuction Team Builder: tabbed

Same data, reorganised. A pinned header keeps budget, standing bid and the clock on screen at all times. The native bottom tab bar (already themed in Theme.swift) splits the rest into Auction (decide now), Table (all rosters) and Board (lot history). The first tab needs no scrolling.

Today: one long column, bid bar below the fold Tabbed: 0 scroll on the bidding tab Tabs: native TabView, bottom Token text: info sheet, not inline

Live on iOSHigher or Lower

Already shipped in MatchView.swift. Header and standings are taken from the code. The card pair is an approximation, since I only read the standings and final sections.

Concept2v2 Wavelength: tabbed

Only two tabs: the game is one board and a score. The header carries both team scores and the clock. Dial changes with your role (psychic, guesser, or watching). Rounds keeps the history of clues, with each target revealed once its round ends.

Source: Theme.swift tokens, AuctionMatchView.swift, MatchView.swift; rules from docs/games/*/rules.md. Parity note: per AGENTS.md, a game on iOS needs a features.yaml entry and a Jira ticket before it ships.
